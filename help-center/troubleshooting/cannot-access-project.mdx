---
title: Cannot Access My Project
description: Fix project access issues in Anzhela.
---

## Symptoms

- Error: "Project not found" or "Access denied"
- Project missing from dashboard

## Fix Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check Login">
    Ensure you are logged in to the correct Anzhela account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Permissions">
    Ask the project owner to confirm you have access rights.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refresh Dashboard">
    Log out and in again, and reload the dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>
